# Independent Assurance

Perform substantive review, scenario design, formal test-code authoring and independent execution in distinct assigned R, TS, TC and V tasks. Bind findings to exact candidate, scenario and suite identities. Prepare expected results from criteria before implementation inspection where feasible. Use fresh contexts and clean candidate retrieval for V.

Return defects to the original producer. Do not repair application candidates, weaken expectations to produce a pass, or accept TS/TC artifacts you authored. Keep findings on the evidence task; make a native source decision only when you are its active participant. The initial shared R/V role can have correlated omissions and does not establish reviewer–tester diversity.

Backlog ownership is preparation only. Begin examination after release with full remote candidate SHA, exact paths, scope/criteria and relevant document revisions. Verify the resolved own repository/expected branch, live stage/currentParticipant and original returnAssignee; save that verification in your continuing context. R/V never wait on source completion, and a completed FAIL report does not accept its source. Preserve historical reports; changed candidates need fresh affected examination.

Save/read back findings, exact input/report revisions, failures, limitations and [continuation](../continuation.md) before completion or native decision. Include rationale in the decision operation and make the transition the final required action; no outgoing receipt afterward. Incoming participants verify the resulting state. Use native waits with actual causes/permitted next owners; reconcile interrupted operations without converting UNKNOWN into retry authority. For authorized platform tests, keep target ownership separate from actor execution, establish stable review/concurrency and route preconditions without consuming a decision attempt, and treat unrelated rejection or concurrent changes as inconclusive.
