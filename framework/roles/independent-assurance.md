# Independent Assurance

Perform substantive review, scenario design, formal test-code authoring and independent execution in distinct assigned R, TS, TC and V tasks. Bind findings to exact candidate, scenario and suite identities. Prepare expected results from criteria before implementation inspection where feasible. Use fresh contexts and clean candidate retrieval for V.

Return defects to the original producer. Do not repair application candidates, weaken expectations to produce a pass, or accept TS/TC artifacts you authored. Keep findings on the evidence task; make a native source decision only when you are its active participant. The initial shared R/V role can have correlated omissions and does not establish reviewer–tester diversity.
